Health data · Hackachusetts 2026 · Oct 2026

Optics: Research Gap Index

Compares how common a disease is among Massachusetts adults with how many clinical studies are recruiting for it in the state. It shows where research lags behind the number of people affected.

MA studies per 100k affected adults (log scale)
High cholesterol
0.25
Asthma
1.22
Diabetes
11.02
  • Python pipeline joins ClinicalTrials.gov studies with CDC PLACES prevalence; each score is a range from CDC's confidence limits.
  • I wrote the dashboard spec and the scoring export that feeds the site. The export has 350+ tests.
  • Built by a 4-person team on parallel spec branches, with every number linked to its source study.

Data science · R · April 2026

Medtrics: elderly fracture outcomes

🏅 Best in Storytelling with Data · ASA DataFest

Four years of de-identified hospital data, one question: does activating a patient portal help elderly fracture patients survive?

Mortality, patients 65+
No MyChart
23.3%
MyChart active
0.14%
  • Built 3 logistic regression models on 17,446 patients (pseudo R² = 0.45).
  • A Baron-Kenny mediation test moved the odds ratio only −0.74%, so the portal's effect is direct.
  • Projected $4.6M in avoided readmissions if 10% of inactive patients sign up.

CourtVision

🏆 Best AI/ML Hack

An NBA platform for new fans: tonight's game predictions, AI scouting reports, and a fact-checker for sports-media hot takes. Built in 24 hours.

  • Trained XGBoost on 3 seasons of game logs; served predictions in <200ms with Redis.
  • An LLM on Groq (GPT-OSS 120B) writes player scouting reports and grades media claims against the stats.
